# Artifact Acquisition

All required executable public-data modules were downloaded, installed, or trained from the public matrix.

Private gp.sa.core/gstore IO and the exact custom gene-set revision are not asserted to be public artifacts. The R adapter reconstructs their input from public counts; exact numerical equivalence is not claimed.

Weights, count matrices, fold latents and transition kernels persist on the Network Volume and are not committed to GitHub. Rebuild with the complete-study command in the runbook.
